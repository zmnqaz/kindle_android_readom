WeChat Read on the Kindle 10th generation

Android 8.1 brought to the Kindle 10th generation: the official e-ink edition of WeChat Read, audiobooks over Bluetooth headphones, Wi‑Fi and deep sleep all run on the real device, and the stock Kindle system stays where it is. The Kindle 10th generation is the Kindle Paperwhite 4 (PW4), released in 2018.

I
Chapter one

What already works on the 10th generation

Every item below has run on a real Kindle 10th generation.

01Android 8.1

Readom’s Android 8.1 (AOSP) system on the 10th generation’s own 4.1 kernel, with the display, touch, Wi‑Fi and power hardware wired up one by one.

02Official e-ink WeChat Read

Tencent’s official e-ink edition of WeChat Read installs and works, for reading and for listening.

03Audiobooks over Bluetooth

Bluetooth is new in this port: pair headphones and WeChat Read’s audiobooks play through them (A2DP, SBC). The original Readom has no Bluetooth.

04Wi‑Fi and deep sleep

Wi‑Fi works. Press the power button and the device goes into deep sleep; press it again to wake, and Wi‑Fi reconnects by itself.

05E-ink refresh fixed

The 10th generation’s display driver was being sent the wrong waveforms; that is fixed. Turning a page while an audiobook plays, the refresh starts after about 0.5 s and finishes after about 1 s.

06The stock system stays

No stock partition is written: all of Android lives in image files on the Kindle’s drive. The stock Kindle system is kept and you can switch back to it at any time.

From lifting the finger to the screen starting to change, about 0.5 s; the whole page is done after about 1 s. About 0.5 s of that is the e-ink greyscale waveform itself.

Conditions: turning pages by hand while an audiobook plays, after the refresh fix.

Storage fix: read errors after repeated sleep and wake

Before (an interim build)

171

After repeated sleep and wake, the eMMC returned 171 CRC read errors, and the system UI crashed as a result.

→
After

0

100 sleep and wake cycles, reading 64 MiB and verifying it right after every wake: zero errors.

Both runs were on the same device with different builds. The 100-cycle run used short, timer-woken sleeps; long sleeps have not been tested yet.

IV
Chapter four

Status and known issues

What is not done yet, first.

Done

  • Android 8.1 boots on a real 10th generation; touch, front light and power button work
  • The official e-ink WeChat Read works, audiobooks included
  • Audiobooks over Bluetooth headphones (A2DP, SBC)
  • Wi‑Fi works; deep sleep with the screen off, woken by the power button
  • E-ink refresh fixed: about 0.5 s until a page starts to refresh, about 1 s until it is done
  • Storage fixed: 100 sleep and wake cycles with read verification, zero errors
  • The stock Kindle system is kept, no stock partition is written, and you can switch back at any time

Not done yet

  • The one-step installer is being built. For now the port starts the way developers start it, so it cannot be installed by ordinary users yet; downloads and a guide are in preparation.
  • It is not smooth. The 10th generation has a single 1 GHz core, 512 MB of memory and no GPU, so the CPU draws everything. WeChat Read is usable, but turning pages and switching screens are slow, more so while an audiobook plays.
  • A jailbreak is required. Jailbreaking is not part of this project; judge the risk for yourself.
  • Refresh slows down on a low battery. At 20% and below, the stock display driver throttles itself and pages turn more slowly; charge first if you want to judge the speed.

Install coming soon

Two ways to install, same result; pick either. The one-step installer is still being built; the file, where to download it and the space it needs will be published here.

Way one

Let an AI do it

Send the text below to any AI assistant. It does the computer side by following these instructions (in Chinese): clearing leftover updates, filling the drive for a while so the Kindle cannot update itself, removing the filler after the jailbreak, downloading and verifying the installer, copying it over and ejecting. You plug and unplug the cable and tap a few things on the Kindle.

Please read https://zmnqaz.github.io/readom/install_pw4.md and follow it to jailbreak my Kindle 10th generation (Paperwhite 4) and install Readom on it. The document is in Chinese; please talk to me in English.
Whatever you can do on the computer yourself, just do it and tell me the result; do not turn it into steps for me.
For anything I have to do on the Kindle, give me one step at a time and wait for my reply.
If you cannot open the link, tell me and I will paste the document.

What you do on the Kindle: switch airplane mode on and off; on the jailbreak web page, wait for the scan and type in the code it shows; tap Settings → Help → Getting Started; finally tap Readom in the library.

Until the installer is published, an AI reading the instructions will tell you so and leave your Kindle alone.

Way two

Step by step yourself

  1. Stop automatic updatesTurn on airplane mode, connect to a computer, delete leftover update*.bin files in the root of the Kindle drive, and fill the drive for now until only 50–90 MB are free.
  2. JailbreakUse Sanctuary in the Kindle’s own browser: after the page’s scan, type in the code it shows, then tap Settings → Help → Getting Started. If it fails, try SpiderCat.
  3. Remove the filler, copy one fileAfter the jailbreak, delete the filler files, copy the installer into the drive’s documents folder, eject and unplug.
  4. Tap it in the libraryTap Readom in the library; the rest happens on the Kindle.
Installer, download and space needed to be announced

About the jailbreak

The maintainer’s Kindle 10th generation (stock firmware 5.18.1.1.1) was jailbroken with Sanctuary: all on the Kindle, no computer, no Amazon account. The Kindle was on a Wi‑Fi network that could reach sites outside mainland China; whether it works without that has not been verified. If Sanctuary fails, SpiderCat is the fallback.

The jailbreak tools are community work, not part of this project; judge the risk for yourself. Original guides: Sanctuary on KindleModding and Bookfere (Chinese); SpiderCat on KindleModding; for other firmware, the jailbreak wizard.

Credits and notes

with
thanks

Readom is the work of hbyxabc (小蹦大侠 on Bilibili). He brought Android 8.1 to the Kindle Paperwhite 3 and reworked refresh, interface, input method and installation for e-ink, so that a Kindle can run the official e-ink edition of WeChat Read while keeping its stock system.

This port stands on his repository: we forked Readom, moved it to the Kindle 10th generation with that device’s kernel and hardware support, and added audiobooks over Bluetooth. Most of the design and code in the system comes from the original project.

For the original Readom — downloads, guides, problems — go to the original project. For problems with the 10th-generation port, open an issue in this fork, please, not with the original author.
